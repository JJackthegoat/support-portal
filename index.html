Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a support ticket?

Click Submit a Ticket on the homepage and fill out the support request form.

Your request can be reviewed by a support agent once a ticket management system is connected.

How do I get help?

Use the Submit a Ticket page to describe your issue and the help you need.
